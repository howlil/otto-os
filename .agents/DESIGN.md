## Direction

Otto is a Windows shell surface borrowing the spatial confidence of macOS without pretending to own external application state. Its character is quiet, precise, and responsive. Its world is a layered desktop with one native source of truth for each window and a temporary captured visual layer for Genie motion. Its signature moment is the application being pulled into its exact Dock tile instead of merely shrinking in place.

Avoid large web-style slides, scale-from-zero windows, decorative bounce on window frames, and competing animation engines.

## Guardrails

External HWND lifecycle state remains native Win32. Otto may mask minimize and restore with one temporary captured snapshot overlay; close remains native and does not route through the Dock.

Motion values live in `otto-core::motion`; app and render layers do not duplicate timing policy.

Every transition is time-based, reversible from its current value, and safe to settle immediately when reduced motion is enabled. Genie locks one Dock target, uses shared strip boundaries on one bounded overlay, and restores through the exact inverse path.

## Patterns

The top-right cluster reads left to right as Tray Overflow, Wi-Fi, Volume, Battery, Search, Control Center, Notifications, and Date/Time. Each action owns one hit rectangle and one hover, pressed, open, or focus treatment; no control uses a permanent pill. Date/time opens only the calendar, while the bell opens grouped notifications. Only one Otto popup or Control Center surface remains open, and any action hidden by width pressure stays reachable through explicit overflow.

Notch media controls (Music tab) hit at 24 px minimum; the visual track may be smaller and is centered in its hit rect. Controls in the expanded notch keep at least 4 px between hit rects and never cross the notch curve. Dimmed or unsupported controls must name the reason, and "engaged" means hovered or keyboard-focused, so the reason reaches keyboard users too.

Expanded Music subtitle priority: an engaged unsupported control's reason (up to two lines, replacing the equalizer), then the active lyric in the cover accent, then the artist ("Paused · artist" while paused). The lyrics drawer does not suppress the header line.

Icons are drawn vector glyphs. A text character never stands in for an icon.

Lyrics drawer follows the Apple Music reading model in a bordered card: a 14 pt rounded card (5 % fill, 10 % frame, 102 pt tall at 14 pt vertical-balanced inset and 16 pt top/bottom edge fade) holds the rows, the active line sits on the vertical centre without a highlight background pill, text is left-aligned with a 14 pt inset, and only three rows are visible (previous, active, next). The active line is 16 pt bold; neighbours use a continuous smoothstep opacity (38 % at one row away, 0 % by two rows) and normalized concentric soft blur (up to 2.2 px at 96 dpi, blurring smoothly once focus distance exceeds 0.18 while capping incoming pre-active rows cleanly so rows transition without a brightness pop or card-edge crowding). A line that fits stays on one line, a line up to 14% too wide shrinks to fit, and only a longer line wraps (two lines at most). Scroll keeps the active row on the centre using each row's measured height and anticipates each timestamp by 120 ms with a 120 ms settle glide (clamped monotonically for fast lines), while controller wakeups target exact lyric timestamps and a 120 ms sync drift ceiling. The active line fills left to right with a 10 pt feathered edge across its gap to the next line (capped at 6 s), continuing line by line when it wraps. Scroll and fill derive from the playback position, so seeking needs no extra state. Reduced motion shows the active line fully bright with no glide or fill. Reference recreations of Apple Music (am-lyrics, SwiftUI clones) anchor the active line near the top; this card keeps the active line centered without a pill box so gradient blur and scale hold hierarchy cleanly.

Notch morphs: switching Focus and Music slides the two content groups 24 pt and crossfades them; opening the lyrics drawer interpolates the notch height, the drawer, and the seek bar (the hero and transport stay put); opening the panel rises its content 8 pt as the shape grows. Rates live in `MotionMetrics`. Reduced motion jumps every morph to its target.

Pomodoro: one gradient per phase (Focus orange, Short Break green to teal, Long Break cyan to blue) colours the ring, compact time, segments, and primary button; the panel time stays white. Pause removes saturation and glow. The last minute of a focus session turns the time and ring coral and lights a thin rim that pulses once per second. Compact (292 pt): a ring with a soft glow on the left and a 13 px time on the right. The middle hosts 2.5 s peeks (session started, paused, +5 min added, 5 minutes left) that fade in and out. When both Focus and Music are active in compact mode, the active primary module renders its full single-activity layout in the Main Notch (`layout.notch`) while the secondary module detaches into an Obsidian Black circle (`layout.notch_secondary`: `32 x 32 pt` matching Dynamic Island height when floating, or `28 x 28 pt` inside the `32 pt` flush top bar) 8 pt to the right of the Main Notch; clicking the secondary circle swaps the two modules (`compact_swap_blend`) and syncs the expanded panel tab to the Main Notch module, while right-clicking either slot dismisses that slot independently. Panel (380 x 144 pt, 16 pt inset, three rows): header (Focus, Break or Rest tab beside Music, and the session label), hero (48 pt phase icon, 30 px time, derived subtitle such as "Deep focus · ends 12:48", then reset, a 40 pt primary, and skip), and progress (four session segments while focusing, one continuous bar on breaks, with the start time and the next break). Idle focus shows the 15, 25, 45 and 60 minute presets in place of progress; scrolling stays as fine-tune. Completion shows one card in the next phase's colours with +5 min and Start break or Start focus. Phase glyphs (tomato, cup, moon, check, clock) are drawn vectors. The glow breathes over 4 s only while a session runs; reduced motion keeps glow and pulse static and shows peeks without fades. Copy and the surface for each state are resolved in `otto-core::pomodoro_view`, rects in `otto-core::layout`; render only draws them.

Music: the cover's dominant colour (pink when there is no cover or it is grey) tints the cover glow, the ambient panel wash, the play button, and the seek fill; text stays neutral. Panel (380 x 144 pt, same 16 pt grid as Pomodoro): header (Focus and Music tabs, the source label such as Spotify or Browser, and a 24 pt lyrics toggle drawn as a vector quote mark), hero (48 pt rounded cover with a soft halo, 15 px title, 11 px artist, a small equalizer, then previous, a 40 pt primary, and next), and progress (a 4 px seek bar with elapsed and remaining time; hover adds a 10 px thumb and lifts the fill 14 %). Paused dims the cover to 55 %, drops the glow, turns the equalizer into four dots, and reads "Paused · artist". A source without a length shows LIVE and a soft segment drifting along the bar instead of a fill. Opening lyrics grows the panel to 254 pt with the 102 pt drawer between hero and seek bar (balancing top and bottom padding around three rows with the 14 pt side inset). Compact (292 pt base): symmetrical edge anchors pair a 20 pt left cover ring with a tight 16 pt right equalizer slot (expanding to 34 pt only for the red `LIVE` badge via `media_live`), keeping the centered title, synced lyric line, or centered peek (`label + title`) equidistant between cover and wave. A pause keeps the compact island for 8 s with a dimmed cover and a flat equalizer, then it collapses. 2.5 s peeks (Now playing, Paused, Playing, Skipped, each as a muted label before the title) replace the title when something happens and are not shown while the Pomodoro owns the compact island. Reduced motion shows peeks without fades and parks the live segment in the middle. Copy, the subtitle choice, and the dominant colour are resolved in `otto-core::media_view` and `otto-core::cover_color`, rects in `otto-core::layout`; render only draws them.

Privacy indicators: active microphone (orange) or camera (green) capture places 6 pt indicator dots in `layout.privacy_dots` beside the bar notch (clicking opens and pins the notch panel, switching from Focus or absent Media to Pocket so the mic control is immediately visible). While microphone/camera is active or the mic is muted, Pocket (`40 pt` compact), Clip (`40 pt` compact), and Music (`88 pt` labeled pill with app name) surface a header `PrivacyMicMute` toggle pill.

Pocket (File Drop Shelf, expanded panel tab): holds up to 12 items (`MAX_SHELF_ITEMS`) across two 3x2 card pages (`SHELF_PAGE_SIZE = 6`) inside a 90 pt high card body (`SHELF_BODY_HEIGHT_POINTS = 90`). Header holds optional page indicator (`1/2`), a 40 pt vector-plus `Add` pill, and (when populated) a 38 pt red-tinted `Clear` pill. Empty state draws a dashed rounded dropzone (`Drop files, links, or text here`, switching to `Release to keep in Pocket` and blue highlight on OLE drag-hover) and opens the file picker on click; populated state renders 3x2 cards with square thumbnail/glyph tiles (up to 128 px Shell thumbnails cached per item or drawn vector glyphs for 10 `ShelfKindTag` kinds), label, and 18 pt Copy and Remove vector mini-buttons. Left-click opens files/URLs or copies text; drag past `SM_CXDRAG`/`SM_CYDRAG` starts an outbound OLE drag (`CF_HDROP` / `CF_UNICODETEXT` / URL); right-click reveals files in Explorer or copies links/text. Drag-entering the notch or open panel switches to Pocket and uses a 220 ms `DragLeave` grace across the dual-HWND notch boundary; pointer-leave collapse stays suppressed while drag-hover, drag-opened inspection, modal file picking, or outbound drag holds.

Clipboard (Clip, expanded panel tab beside Pocket): holds up to 12 ephemeral RAM-only recent copies (`MAX_CLIPBOARD_ITEMS = 12`) across 4-row pages (`CLIPBOARD_PAGE_SIZE = 4`) inside a 90 pt high body. Header holds an optional page indicator (`1/3`), a `Live`/`Paused` capture pill, a `Clear` pill (when populated), and the Privacy mic control when active; each row displays a vector kind glyph (`Text`, `Link`, or `Files`), a sanitized single-line preview, compact byte/item metadata, transient `Copied` visual confirmation on click, and a trailing remove `×` button. Sensitive and opt-out clipboard formats are skipped.

Transient HUD (OS events) uses one template for every event: glyph, neutral label, meter, value, in fixed columns of the 356 pt pill. Labels stay neutral; accent colour belongs only to the glyph, meter, and value. Green means charging or Caps Lock on, amber means low battery, and muted means off or muted. Muted volume reads as the word "Muted" with an empty meter. Caps Lock shows a state pill and never a meter. The meter is a continuous 4 pt bar, not segments. Content fades in after the shape has mostly grown (`hud_content_alpha`), and each new event restarts the hold (`MotionMetrics::hud_hold_ms`). Presentation and geometry are resolved in `otto-core::hud`; render only draws them.

Liquid glass (bar, Dock, Control Center, and Popup Menu) is one adaptive Clear material, not a user-selectable Light/Dark/Opaque style. Each active surface samples its backdrop, applies a `12 pt` soft blur, and uses average luminance with `0.44`/`0.56` hysteresis to select white or graphite ink without flicker. The glass body keeps low tint (`0.05` Dark-ink / `0.07` graphite-ink), chroma-preserving grading, a bright legibility floor for graphite ink (`0.52`), curved Fresnel rims, directional dual specular catchlights, inner caustic glow, and a proportional convex bezel (`min(lens_band, 44%` of the short half-axis) whose Snell-style refraction and RGB dispersion stay concentrated near the meniscus while the body remains softly frosted. Top bar glass bleeds 22 pt past top/left/right edges so only its bottom edge refracts. Windows Reduce Transparency, unavailable blur, and high contrast still select opaque accessibility fallbacks; the legacy `theme_mode` and `liquid_glass` config keys remain readable for compatibility but no longer override or expose appearance controls.

Control Center uses Clear Layered Glass in a `376 pt` Main Apple Bento shell on a strict `4 px` grid (`22 pt` shell radius, `14 pt` card radius, `12 pt` outer pad, `8 pt` Bento gutter): top-left `Connectivity` (`144 pt`) holds Wi-Fi & Bluetooth (`32 pt` circular switches, inset hairline separator, chevron openers); top-right stacks full-width `Focus` and `Battery` cards at matching heights, with Focus opening `Otto Settings` when disabled and keeping a distinct corner settings action. There is no Appearance card, glass toggle, or theme strip. Middle `64 pt` rows render `Display` (`328x26` full-width capsule brightness slider with leading Sun glyph) and aligned `Sound` (`26x26` circular `SoundMuteToggle` on the left column + `8 pt` gap + smooth capsule slider, zero duplicate speaker glyph or header pill clutter). Bottom rows render `74 pt` `Now Playing` (weighted Play/Pause focal button vs light Prev/Next glyphs) and `46 pt` `"Otto Modules & Settings"` (`8`-tooth machined cog + quiet right-aligned `"{active}/{total} active"` tabular count), opening proportional `20/24/34/22%` 4-segment (`All | Modules | Notch & Dock | System`) centered-tab **grouped inset subpanels** with a reserved `8 pt` right scrollbar gutter and unclipped `14 pt` rounded group bottoms, inset `1 px` dividers, `9.5 pt` dependency badges (`Requires Dock`, `Requires Media`), inline error banner, tactile `SetCapture` press/cancel feedback, and keyboard/UIA parity.

## State

Platform observation owns the truth about external windows. App state owns local animation progress. Core owns interpolation and resolved geometry.

Closing feedback must recover if the target window remains visible, for example because the application opened an unsaved-changes dialog.

## Accessibility

Read Windows client-area animation and UI-effects preferences. Reduced motion removes bounce and interpolation while preserving the same actions and final states.

Notch controls join the bar keyboard order only while the expanded panel is open: the Focus (`Pomodoro`), `Shelf` (`Pocket`), and `Clipboard` (`Clip`) tabs when their respective module is enabled, the `Media` tab and media controls only when the media module is enabled and media is present, and `PrivacyMicMute` whenever its pill is shown. Collapsed `Notch`, active `NotchSecondary`, and `PrivacyIndicator` also participate in bar traversal; activating collapsed `Notch` opens the panel and moves focus to the active tab strip. On the Focus tab the Focus sub-mode selectors, presets (idle focus/timer), reset, play or pause, and skip are reachable; while the session-complete card shows, only +5 min and the next-phase button are. On the Pocket tab pager (when multi-page), Add, Clear (when populated), visible card tiles, and their per-card copy and remove actions are reachable; on the Clipboard tab pager (when multi-page), Pause/Live, Clear (when populated), visible rows, and row remove buttons are reachable. Tabs, lyrics toggle, previous, play/pause, next, and the seekbar are reachable on the Media tab; Left and Right seek by 5 s while the seekbar is focused. Each focused control draws a focus ring, activates through the same path as a click, and is exposed through UI Automation (`WM_GETOBJECT` / `IInvokeProvider`) across the Bar, Notch panel, Dock, and Control Center.
